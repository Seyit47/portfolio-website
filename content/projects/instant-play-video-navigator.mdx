## Context

A small, focused product question: can a web page make video feel as immediate as a native
app on a phone?

## What I built

A TypeScript React application with React Router, a `VideoContext` that owns loading state and
progress, a landing page that preloads, and a player page that plays. A service worker
(`video-sw.js`) caches the media.

## Decisions

- Preload on the landing screen where the viewer is reading anyway, and show honest progress.
- Keep the player tiny; the win comes from caching, not from a feature-rich player.
- Ship Docker and Netlify configs so the app can be hosted either way.

## Result

A demo-ready pattern for instant video on mobile that can be lifted into larger products.
